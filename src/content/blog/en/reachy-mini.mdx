## 🌟 Reachy Mini's Birth Story: From 3D-Printed Prototype to 3000-Unit Production Miracle

Reachy Mini didn't appear out of thin air; it's the brainchild of French robotics company **Pollen Robotics**. Since 2016, Pollen has focused on open-source practical robots emphasizing Human-Robot Interaction. Later acquired by AI giant **Hugging Face** (🤗), Reachy Mini became their first open-source desktop robot.

- **July 2025**: Debuted as a 3D-printed prototype. Lite $299, Full $449 pre-orders. Early development, inviting user feedback.
- **Late 2025**: Orders exploded! 3000+ in a week, rushing from prototype to mass production.
- **CES 2026**: Partnered with **Seeed Studio** (global open hardware leader), shipped 3000 units. NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang highlighted it in keynote, signaling AI robots for the masses.

This "open journey" is like what Pollen founder Matthieu Lapeyre shares in the interview video:

## 🚀 Getting Started: From Assembly to AI Creation

Reachy Mini is a **DIY kit**—assemble with friends/kids for fun! Official interactive guide: [Hugging Face Assembly](https://huggingface.co/spaces/pollen-robotics/Reachy_Mini_Assembly_Guide).

### 1. **Software Install** (5 mins)
```
pip install reachy-mini  # Or uv for speed
```

### 2. **Hello World** (Python SDK)
```python
from reachy_mini import ReachyMini
from reachy_mini.utils import create_head_pose

with ReachyMini() as mini:
    # Look up and tilt
    mini.goto_target(
        head=create_head_pose(z=10, roll=15, degrees=True),
        duration=1.0
    )
```

### 3. **Plug-and-Play Apps** (Hugging Face Ecosystem)
- **Conversation App**: Chat with LLMs.
