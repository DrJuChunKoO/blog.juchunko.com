# 🪙 NadMail: Every Email is an Investment

**Imagine this: when you send someone an email, you're actually investing in them.**

This isn't a metaphor. On [NadMail](https://nadmail.ai), every email comes with a micro-investment: 0.001 MON automatically purchases the recipient's token. The more people email you, the more valuable your token becomes.

**Your social graph IS your market cap.**

## 🏗️ Technical Architecture: Social Investment Infrastructure on Monad

NadMail cleverly designed an automated investment system:

### Registration Flow
1. **Register** `handle@nadmail.ai`
2. **Auto-create** meme coin on [nad.fun](https://nad.fun)
3. **Link** email ↔ token relationship

### Email Flow
1. **Compose** email to someone
2. **Auto-purchase** 0.001 MON of recipient's token
3. **Send** email + on-chain transaction record

### Economic Model
```
Social Value = Σ(emails received × 0.001 MON)
Market Cap = token holders × average holdings × current price
Social Graph = your investor network
```
